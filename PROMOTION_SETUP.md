# Promotion and Launch Setup

This site is being built for two things at the same time:

1. repeat visits from small service businesses that need to price jobs often
2. free discovery from search, shareable guides, and practical tool use

## Reliable free promotion channels

These are the best free channels to prioritize for this specific site model:

- Google Search via long-tail guides and tool-focused homepage linking
- Bing Search via Bing Webmaster Tools submission
- Pinterest via vertical pins built from estimate templates, checklists, and pricing examples
- Reddit only where helpful, such as small business or trade-specific discussions, without spammy link dropping
- Facebook groups for painters, cleaners, lawn care operators, and pressure washing businesses if posts are genuinely useful
- Direct outreach to solo operators you know later, asking what calculator or template should be added next

## Why these channels are realistic

- Search is realistic because the site already has a homepage tool, guide hub, sitemap, robots file, canonicals, and focused service pages.
- Pinterest is realistic because service templates, estimate checklists, and pricing visuals are naturally pin-friendly and can keep bringing traffic over time.
- Bing is worth doing because submission is free and often easier to win on early than Google.
- Niche communities can work if the post solves a real problem first and the link is secondary.

## Promotion plan after launch

### Week 1

- Submit the live site to Google Search Console
- Submit the sitemap to Bing Webmaster Tools
- Publish 3 to 5 Pinterest pins that point to the strongest guide pages
- Add one short launch post to your own social profile describing the free quote workbench

### Week 2 to 4

- Publish 1 new long-tail guide each week
- Add internal links from every guide back to the homepage workbench
- Post 1 useful example thread or answer in one relevant community each week
- Watch which service page gets the most interest and expand that cluster first

## Content angles that are most promotable

- painting estimate examples
- house cleaning deep clean vs recurring pricing
- pressure washing minimum charge examples
- lawn care route pricing and recurring invoice examples
- quote wording templates that reduce customer confusion

## Before turning on AdSense

- replace the placeholder publisher ID in `script.js`
- replace the placeholder line in `ads.txt`
- review `privacy/index.html` again so it matches your live ad and analytics setup
- make sure the homepage tool and guide pages have enough original content and are not thin

## Notes

- Do not rely on random blog comments or mass link dropping. Those channels are low quality and usually not durable.
- This site should win by being genuinely useful first, then easy to discover, then easy to revisit.
