About the project

Practical quoting help for small service businesses

JobQuote Kit helps small U.S. service businesses prepare clearer quotes, pricing notes, and customer documents. Use the workbench for quick numbers, then use the guides when you need stronger wording or a more complete template.

Reviewed on May 12, 2026 Static, fast, and mobile-ready by design

What we publish

Pages cover quote templates, sample wording, price list formats, measurement notes, margin checks, job add-ons, and printable customer-facing documents.

Who it serves

The target audience is owner-operators and small teams in painting, cleaning, lawn care, pressure washing, handyman work, and closely related service trades across the United States.

How to use the tools

Start with the homepage workbench, load an example, replace the numbers with your job details, then copy or email the summary when the price looks right.

How to use the guides

Open a guide when you need clearer scope language, add-on ideas, invoice structure, or a more polished customer explanation before sending a quote.
