About the project

Why this site exists

JobQuote Kit is built for a simple but valuable search intent: small U.S. home service businesses need better pricing examples, estimate structures, and invoice templates. Instead of vague advice, the site focuses on practical pages operators can actually use before sending a quote or collecting payment.

Reviewed on May 12, 2026 Static, fast, and mobile-ready by design

What we publish

The project now combines repeat-use pricing tools with supporting guides. Pages cover templates, sample wording, price list formats, measurement methods, margin protection, job add-ons, and printable customer-facing documents.

Who it serves

The target audience is owner-operators and small teams in painting, cleaning, lawn care, pressure washing, handyman work, and closely related service trades across the United States.

Why static pages

A static site is fast, easy to host, and reliable for organic growth. It also makes it possible to ship useful tool workflows without managing a backend or user database.

How the site grows

Each high-quality page becomes a hub for related long-tail topics, while the homepage tool gives those visitors a practical reason to return. One painting estimate guide can later connect to pages about exterior repainting, deck staining, apartment turnover, and cabinet painting.
