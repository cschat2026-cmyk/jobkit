Contact JobQuote Kit

Use this page for support, feedback, privacy questions, or requests for new templates and guides. The email draft includes the site name and page details so messages stay easier to sort later.

Email

Send questions, feedback, and requests to cschat2026@gmail.com.

Good reasons to reach out

Ask for a missing guide, report a confusing quote step, request a clearer template format, or send a privacy-related request.

What to include

If possible, mention the job type, what you were trying to do, and what felt unclear. That makes it easier to improve the right page or feature.

Reply expectations

Messages are reviewed to improve the tool flow, templates, and guide coverage. Include enough detail for the request or issue to be understood from the email alone.
