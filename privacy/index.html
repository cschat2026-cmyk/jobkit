Site policy

Privacy policy

This page explains how JobQuote Kit handles saved tool inputs, advertising technologies, and contact requests.

Last updated May 27, 2026 No account required to use the tools Saved quotes stay in your browser

Information collected

The quote workbench stores saved pricing defaults and saved quote history in the visitor's own browser using local storage. The site does not require account creation to use the tools.

Cookies and advertising

JobQuote Kit uses Google AdSense and is prepared for Ezoic-managed ad delivery when approved. These advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads, measure performance, and limit duplicate impressions.

Email contact

When a visitor taps one of the contact buttons, their email app opens with a prefilled draft that includes the site name, page title, and page URL. That information is included to help organize and review feedback by page.

Contact

Questions, suggestions, and privacy requests can be sent to cschat2026@gmail.com or through the contact page.
