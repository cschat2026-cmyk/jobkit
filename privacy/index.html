Privacy policy

This privacy page explains the current front-end tool behavior, future advertising readiness, and how visitors can contact the site owner with questions or requests.

Reviewed on May 12, 2026 Prepared for future Google advertising disclosures

Information collected

JobQuote Kit is currently a static front-end website. The quote workbench stores saved pricing defaults and saved quote history in the visitor's own browser using local storage so repeat visits are easier. If analytics are added later, this page should be updated to explain what usage data is collected and how it is used.

Cookies and advertising

The site is prepared for future Google AdSense integration. If ads are activated, Google and other advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on prior visits to this and other websites. Before turning ads on, this page should be updated again with your live publisher details and any consent flow required for the regions where you receive traffic.

Email contact

When a visitor taps one of the contact buttons, their email app opens with a prefilled draft that includes the site name, page title, and page URL. That information is included to help organize and review feedback by page later.

Contact

Questions, suggestions, and privacy requests can be sent to cschat2026@gmail.com.
